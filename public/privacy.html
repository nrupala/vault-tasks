Vault Tasks — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · App version 1.1.9 · Published by 2351485 ALBERTA INC. · Contact: info@aimlds.org

The short version

Vault Tasks has no account, no sign-up, and no developer server. We collect no personal data, we share no personal data, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Everything you enter stays on your device, encrypted.

What the app stores, and where

Your tasks and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local database (IndexedDB). Task content is encrypted before it is stored using AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is derived from the master password you choose (PBKDF2, 600,000 iterations) and never leaves your device. We never see your password, your key, or your data, and we could not read your vault if we wanted to.

If you lose your master password, your data cannot be recovered — by you or by us. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, deletes your vault.

Things you can choose to do with your data

  • Export / import: Only when you tap export, the app writes a file (JSON, CSV, or TXT) to a place you choose. Exported files contain your decrypted data in plain form — treat them like the sensitive information they are. Import reads only the file you select.
  • Share / copy: Only when you tap share or copy on an item, that item's content goes to the app, person, or clipboard you choose. Nothing is shared automatically.
  • Voice input: Only when you tap the microphone, the app uses the speech-recognition service built into your browser or device WebView. That service is provided by your browser or device vendor and handles audio under its own privacy policy. Vault Tasks itself does not record, store, or transmit your audio.
  • Notifications: Vault Tasks does not send push notifications and has no notification server. The app may ask the browser or WebView for notification permission when the task view opens; in this release it does not schedule or send notifications, and no data leaves your device for notifications.
  • Support links: Links to our support and sponsorship pages open only when you tap them, in your browser.

Network requests the app makes

The app loads its Inter typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). That request goes to Google and carries standard network information (such as your IP address), as any web request does. No task data, password, or vault content is included in that request. The app makes no other requests to developer or analytics servers.

Children

Vault Tasks is a general productivity app. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the app version and effective date above will change with it.
